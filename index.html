Golden Transit Rule

ONE card covers the entire family — tap sequentially for each member.

Buy one anonymous Istanbulkart at any yellow/blue Biletmatik kiosk. Tap once for Parent 1 and roll the stroller through the wide gate, then pass the same card back for Parent 2. Children under 6 ride 100% free.
